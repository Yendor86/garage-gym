Garage Gym privacy

Last updated 26 August 2026. Garage Gym is a household workout log. It is not a medical device and not for children. There is no social feed.

Operated by Harson Group Pty Ltd, ABN 20 674 654 132 (GST registered).

What we store

On your device and in our Australian cloud (Supabase, Sydney): first name, man/woman colour, household code, equipment (gear) ticks, the sets you log, and optional bodyweight if you type it. We do not sell this data.

What we do not collect

No Google or Apple login. First run does not ask for an email. No health-clinic data. No kids’ accounts. We do not scrape your Play Store or App Store account.

Join

The household code is the join secret. Create a house or type the code. Anyone with that code can join and share the same log.

House League

If a house opts in, House League shows public totals for that house (aggregates such as weekly tonnage and sessions). It is not a feed of other people’s sets or posts.

Delete

Export a backup in More. Leave the household on a device to stop that phone syncing. When in-app delete ships, it will be in More. Until then, email rodney@harsongroup.com.au and we will remove house data we hold.
