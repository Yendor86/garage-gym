Garage Gym privacy

Last updated 24 August 2026. Garage Gym is a household workout logger. It is not a medical device and not for children.

What we store

On your device and in our Australian cloud (Supabase, Sydney): first name, man/woman colour, household code, equipment ticks, and the sets you log. If you type an email when you create a household, we keep it to email you about Garage Gym (updates, later house billing). We do not sell it.

What we do not collect

No Google/Apple login. No health-clinic data. No kids’ accounts. We do not scrape your Play Store or App Store account.

Sync

A household code lets other phones in your house share the same log. Anyone with that code can join the house.

Delete

You can export a backup in More. Leave the household on a device to stop that phone syncing. Full account deletion is not a self-serve button yet — email us from the support page and we will remove house data we hold.
